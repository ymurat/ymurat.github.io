My research interests

What are my research interests?

My research is in software engineering and artificial intelligence. Most of my recent work asks how generative AI and large language models change the way software is specified, built, estimated and maintained, and what practitioners gain or lose in the process. With colleagues in the European software process improvement community I run a series of adapted multivocal literature reviews, which combine academic and grey literature, on AI-based software engineering, context engineering for GenAI-enabled development, automated refactoring, DevOps, DevSecOps, MLOps and CloudOps, low-code and inner source, programming language selection, estimation, and usability measurement.

A second strand concerns immersive environments and digital governance. I study the Metaverse as a setting for public services and citizen participation, including a case study of The Gambia, the training potential of virtual worlds, and emotion and attention in virtual reality using biosignals, eye tracking and explainable AI. Related work looks at digital disinformation and the regulation of socially critical software.

I remain interested in the human side of software development: team dynamics, personality, occupational anxiety and well-being, and the ethical questions raised by AI-based systems, including bias. My earlier work on gamification, serious games, game theory and mechanism design for the software process still shapes how I think about incentives and engagement.

Since July 2026 I have been putting this research into practice at Sanalus.ai, a platform in development for evidence-based assessment of independent and AI-assisted software engineering competence.

Current topics:
- Generative AI and large language models in software engineering
- Context engineering and AI agents for software development
- Multivocal literature reviews and empirical software engineering
- DevOps, MLOps and software process improvement
- Metaverse, virtual reality and digital governance
- Human factors, ethics and bias in AI-based systems
- Gamification, serious games and mechanism design
