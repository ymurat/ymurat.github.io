ARTICLE HEADER

An artificial intelligence-based narrative engine

Dynamic story generation in virtual reality (VR) using an artificial intelligence-based narrative engine

In a computer-generated 3D world, participants explore a story in a personal style of play they wish to proceed. Based on the core experience of the player, a unique and a dynamic story will be created by a narrative engine, which will use artificial intelligence techniques such as deep learning. The research aims to combine artificial intelligence, narration and cognitive sciences into an interactive HCI framework to produce responses to participant’s interplay. The engine will be able to create interactive non-player characters (NPC) with cognitive capability based on their emotional states. These NPCs will integrate into interactive story and narrative plan that should adapt every decision and action of the participants and NPCs.
